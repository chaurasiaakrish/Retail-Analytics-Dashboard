# 📊 Retail Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Retail Analytics Dashboard** built to analyze business performance across sales, profit, customers, regions, categories, and shipping modes.

This project demonstrates how Business Intelligence can transform raw retail data into meaningful insights that support data-driven decision-making.

---

# 📌 Dashboard Overview

![Dashboard Overview](Images/Dashboard%20Overview.png)

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall business sales and profit.
- Compare sales performance across different regions.
- Identify the highest-performing customer segments.
- Evaluate profit generated by different product categories.
- Analyze yearly sales trends.
- Identify top customers by sales.
- Compare sales across different shipping modes.

---

# 📈 Dashboard Components

## 1️⃣ Total Sales

Displays the total sales generated across all orders.

---

## 2️⃣ Total Profit

Displays the total profit earned by the business.

---

## 3️⃣ Sales by Region

![Sales By Region](Images/1.%20Sales%20By%20Region.png)

### Key Insight
- West region generated the highest sales.
- South region recorded the lowest sales.

---

## 4️⃣ Sales by Segment

![Sales By Segment](Images/2.%20Sales%20By%20Segment.png)

### Key Insight
- Consumer segment contributed the highest sales.
- Home Office generated the lowest sales.

---

## 5️⃣ Profit by Category

![Profit By Category](Images/3.%20Profit%20By%20Category.png)

### Key Insight
- Technology generated the highest profit.
- Furniture generated the lowest profit.

---

## 6️⃣ Sales by Ship Mode

![Sales By Ship Mode](Images/4.%20Sales%20By%20Ship%20Mode.png)

### Key Insight
- Standard Class accounted for the majority of sales.
- Same Day shipping generated the lowest sales.

---

## 7️⃣ Yearly Sales Trend

![Yearly Sales](Images/5.%20Yearly%20Sales.png)

### Key Insight
- Sales showed consistent growth from 2015 to 2017.

---

## 8️⃣ Top Customers by Sales

![Top Customers](Images/6.%20Top%20Customers%20By%20Sales.png)

### Key Insight
- Sean Miller generated the highest sales.
- Tamara Chand and Raymond Buch were among the top-performing customers.

---

# 💡 Business Insights

- 📍 West region generated the highest sales.
- 👥 Consumer segment was the largest contributor to revenue.
- 💻 Technology category generated the highest profit.
- 🚚 Standard Class was the most preferred shipping mode.
- 📈 Business experienced steady sales growth over the years.
- 🏆 A small group of customers contributed significantly to total sales.
